Terms of Service

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Thanks for choosing Island Handyman. These terms explain how I approach estimates, scheduling, payments and the work I provide. My goal is to keep everything straightforward and clear.

01. Estimates and Pricing

Estimates are based on the information available when we discuss the job. If the work turns out to be different from what was expected, I will discuss any changes in the work or price with you before proceeding.

02. Payment

Payment arrangements will be agreed upon before work begins. Unless otherwise arranged, payment is due when the agreed work is completed. Additional expenses will be discussed with you.

03. Materials and Supplies

Materials may be supplied by Island Handyman or provided by the client, depending on the job. Significant changes in material requirements or costs will be discussed beforehand.

04. Scheduling and Availability

Work dates and arrival times are arranged in advance. Weather, transportation, supply delays or unexpected circumstances may occasionally require changes. If that happens, I will keep you informed.

05. Access and Work Areas

Clients are responsible for arranging reasonable access to the work area and letting me know about known issues that could affect the job.

06. Unexpected Conditions

Some problems only become visible once work has started. If hidden damage, unsafe conditions or additional work is discovered, I will explain the situation and discuss the next steps before carrying out additional work.

07. Workmanship

Work is carried out with reasonable care and attention. If you have a concern about completed work, please contact me so we can discuss it and determine an appropriate solution.

08. Property and Safety

Reasonable care will be taken around your home and property. Clients should keep people clear of active work areas and let me know about relevant hazards or property concerns.

09. Privacy

Information shared with Island Handyman is used to communicate with clients, arrange work, maintain job records and provide services. Client information is not sold.

10. Questions

If anything about these terms or an agreed job is unclear, please get in touch. I am always happy to discuss things directly.

These terms are governed by the laws of Ontario, Canada.
